# Glossary

| Term                                 | Meaning                                                                               | Kid-friendly wording we use                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Sol                                  | A Mars day (24 h 39 min). The story runs from Sol 215 to Sol 230.                     | "Sol" (explained once as "a day on Mars")           |
| Science                              | Points earned from experiments, exploring and sending data home                       | "Coins" 🪙                                          |
| Meter                                | One of the six meters (gauges): power, oxygen, water, food, radiation dose, waste               | the meter's name, e.g. "Oxygen"                      |
| Warning level                        | The value where a meter shows ⚠️ (game rule)                                          | "Low!" or "Too high!"                               |
| Event                                | What happens to the outpost on a sol (a real flare, a dust storm, a leak...)          | the event's title, e.g. "Pipe Leak in Life Support!" |
| kWh (kilowatt-hour)                  | Unit of stored electrical energy                                                      | "kWh", explained in the Power tooltip               |
| kL (kilolitre)                       | 1,000 litres                                                                          | "kL"                                                |
| Life support                         | Systems that make air, water and food                                                 | three meters: "Oxygen", "Water", "Food"              |
| Water recycling                      | Cleaning used water (including sweat and urine) back into drinking water              | "Recycle the water"                                 |
| Shielding                            | Material between the crew and space radiation (soil, water walls, the storm shelter)  | "block radiation", "storm shelter"                  |
| Regolith                             | Loose rock and dust covering Mars                                                     | "soil (regolith)"                                   |
| Electrolysis                         | Splitting water into hydrogen and oxygen with electricity                             | "Make oxygen from water"                            |
| Solar flare                          | A sudden burst of energy from the Sun; classes A, B, C, M, X (each 10 times stronger) | "solar flare"; C small, M medium, X huge            |
| CME (coronal mass ejection)          | A huge cloud of charged particles thrown off the Sun                                  | "a giant Sun burp" (use carefully)                  |
| SEP (solar energetic particle event) | A burst of fast particles that is the main radiation danger in a solar storm          | "radiation storm"                                   |
| mSv (millisievert)                   | Unit of radiation dose that accounts for its effect on the body                       | "radiation dose", shown in mSv on its meter         |
| Dose budget                          | The most radiation we let the crew get on this mission (40 mSv, game rule)            | "radiation limit for the mission"                   |
| Micrometeorite                       | A speck of space rock, smaller than a grain of sand, moving very fast                 | "a tiny space pebble"                               |
| EVA                                  | Extravehicular activity - going outside in a spacesuit                                | "go outside" / "spacewalk"                          |
| DONKI                                | NASA's space weather database                                                         | "NASA's space weather records"                      |
| OSDR                                 | NASA's Open Science Data Repository for space biology                                 | "NASA science about astronaut bodies"               |
